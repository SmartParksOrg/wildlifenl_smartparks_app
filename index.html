About

What WildlifeNL Protect is and where to find more

What this is

WildlifeNL Protect shows the data the WildlifeNL platform holds: the collars and other sensors on animals, their positions and measurements, and the detections of camera traps.

Smart Parks made it to see that the data Smart Parks Protect sends to WildlifeNL arrives, and to give partners a quick look at that data. It only reads; it changes nothing in WildlifeNL.

The app runs in your browser and talks directly to WildlifeNL. What you see depends on the roles of your WildlifeNL account.
