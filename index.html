About

What WildlifeNL Protect is and where to find more

What this is

WildlifeNL Protect shows the data the WildlifeNL platform holds: the animals that carry a collar or another sensor, their positions and measurements, the detections of camera traps, the reports people make and the areas all of this happens in.

Smart Parks made it to see that the data Smart Parks Protect sends to WildlifeNL arrives, and to give partners a quick look at that data. Where your account may, you can also change things: add an animal and put a sensor on it, draw a zone, make a report, or set up an experiment.

The app runs in your browser and talks directly to WildlifeNL. What you see and what you can change depend on the roles of your WildlifeNL account.

Signing out removes the token from this browser. WildlifeNL tokens do not expire, so the same token works again after the next sign-in.

Share my location

Off unless you switch it on. While it is on and this app is open on your screen, it sends your position to WildlifeNL at once and then every 10 minutes, as the WildlifeNL phone apps do. WildlifeNL keeps every position. Researchers can see where you were, and a running experiment can send you a message when you are near an animal.

What your account may read

WildlifeNL decides per role what an account may read. Ask the WildlifeNL administrator for a role you miss.

What your account may change

Every change WildlifeNL accepts, with the roles it asks for on this server. Where a role allows a change, the app shows a button for it on the page it belongs to. Some changes WildlifeNL only carries out for the owner of the record. The changes not in this app are made by other systems, such as a collar that sends its readings or the phone apps.
